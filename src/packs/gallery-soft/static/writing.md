# 静态卡片 (gallery-soft/static)

A card that shows any HTML you write, as it is: nothing on it changes in the page, and it has no script. Use it on a custom page for whatever only shows (a heading for a group of cards, links the user opens often, a short list, a picture, a table of a few rows); make a block of your own only where the card needs behavior or data that changes. Its places are `page` alone: a document already has a block for everything that only shows, and you write its HTML as it is there (writing-html.md), so no document's menu offers it.

It is 柔光风格's (the pack `gallery-soft`, off until the user turns it on in 设置 › 组件列表): the same card as Gallery 风格's `gallery/static`, its settings, its data and what the user does on it the same, in the soft look (a faint wash of blue from the card's top, rounded chips, big numbers). Write it only where the user asked for 柔光风格 or the page already holds its cards.

## How to write it

Put it on a custom page as a card of its board, `<section class="gallery-card" data-card-id="…" data-x data-y data-w data-h>`, holding one instance, after `gallery block add gallery-soft/static`, and write the HTML in the instance's one `<div>`, as example.html does:

- the content, the `div` right inside the instance (its part `content`, edited as `none`): any HTML, headings, paragraphs, lists, links, a table, a picture. No `<script>`, no form fields, no `on…` attributes: the card only shows.
- a picture: a file of the page's `assets/` (`pages/<slug>/assets/`), written `assets/<file>` (a name of letters, digits, dots, dashes and underscores, right in that folder), which the server serves at `/pages/<slug>/assets/<file>`: put the file there first (`gallery page check` says a picture whose file is not there), and never `cover.png` alone, which nothing serves. A small SVG or PNG may be a `data:` address in the `<img>` itself.
- the look: 柔光风格's (How it looks, below), each rule as light as the elements it names, so that a page's own `<style>` or a `style` attribute changes any of it. It sets no color but the links', the chips' and a faint wash of blue from the card's top: its words are the color they stand in, the page's text or the color your page gives the card (`.gallery-card[data-card-id="…"] { background: …; color: … }`) or a wrapper of the content (`style="color: …"`), every heading in it too, and its small words, list dots, quotes and lines a share of that color. Write colors of your own for both themes (`:root[data-doc-effective-theme="dark"]` on a custom page), a link's too where its fill is dark.
- Size: any whole number of cells, 1 to 12 wide and 1 to 24 high, 4 × 2 by default (the 大小 menu lists 4 × 2, 2 × 2, 4 × 4, 8 × 2, 2 × 1 and 4 × 1): write `data-w` and `data-h` to fit what you wrote (`gallery page add <slug> gallery-soft/static --w <w> --h <h>` does too). The user changes it by dragging the card's lower right corner, or from the 大小 menu of the grip at the card's top right, which lists the common sizes. The card does not grow with what it holds: what does not fit scrolls inside it, so give it the height it needs.

An empty one (`<div></div>`, what a menu adds) says that it has nothing yet (还没有内容, 跟 Agent 说要在这里放什么), so that the user asks you for it.

## How it looks

What you wrote, in 柔光风格's soft look: a heading it starts with, where more follows, is the card's head row (a chip of lines in the accent's soft tint and the title; small words in the heading, `<h3>十一月开销 <small>截至 11月12日</small></h3>`, its quiet meta at the end); a paragraph of small words alone right under it a muted subtitle; a list a dot an item; a list whose items are each one link, rows like the sidebar's (`<li><a href="…">Gallery 仓库 <small>github.com</small></a></li>`: a link's picture on a soft square before it, the small words at its end); a last paragraph that is one link, the accent's with an arrow out, at the card's bottom; a table of hairlines, its columns after the first numbers at the right; on a 2 × 2 card what follows the heading at the bottom; on a card one cell high its one line in the middle, a paragraph that is one link after a chip of a page and before an arrow out, a first `<span>` of it (an emoji: `<p><span>📌</span> 这周专心发版</p>`) on a chip. The user changes it only by asking you: to change it, write the HTML in `page.html` again, that card alone, its `data-card-id` and place kept.
