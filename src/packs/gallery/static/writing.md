# 静态卡片 (gallery/static)

A card that shows any HTML you write, as it is: nothing on it changes in the page, and it has no script. Use it on a custom page for whatever only shows (a heading for a group of cards, links the user opens often, a short list, a picture, a table of a few rows); make a block of your own only where the card needs behavior or data that changes. Its places are `page` alone: a document already has a block for everything that only shows, and you write its HTML as it is there (writing-html.md), so no document's menu offers it.

## How to write it

Put it on a custom page as a card of its board, `<section class="gallery-card" data-card-id="…" data-x data-y data-w data-h>`, holding one instance, after `gallery block add static`, and write the HTML in the instance's one `<div>`, as example.html does:

- the content, the `div` right inside the instance (its part `content`, edited as `none`): any HTML, headings, paragraphs, lists, links, a table, a picture. No `<script>`, no form fields, no `on…` attributes: the card only shows.
- a picture: a file of the page's `assets/` (`pages/<slug>/assets/`), written `assets/<file>` (a name of letters, digits, dots, dashes and underscores, right in that folder), which the server serves at `/pages/<slug>/assets/<file>`: put the file there first (`gallery page check` says a picture whose file is not there), and never `cover.png` alone, which nothing serves. A small SVG or PNG may be a `data:` address in the `<img>` itself.
- the look: the card gives it a light one (Gallery 风格's: 13px words on 20px lines) that weighs nothing, so that a page's own `<style>` or a `style` attribute changes any of it: a heading the content starts with, where more follows it, is drawn as the cards' head row (13px, 600, a small chip of lines before it), so that the card sits among Gallery's own; a list's items stand after small dots; a small heading (`<h4>`) is a faint label over what follows (常用); a paragraph of one link is a row with a small picture before it (a page for one of the workspace's, a link's for the web) and its `<small>` at its end, for a domain or a date: `<p><a href="https://www.npmjs.com/…">npm · @heycys/gallery</a><small>npmjs.com</small></p>`; a paragraph of `<strong>` words alone is a line larger (15px), and right after the heading the content starts with it stands at the card's foot with what follows it, a small widget's figure (`<h3>发布前</h3><p><strong>还差 2 项</strong></p><p><small>门禁 · 盲测</small></p>`); a table is compact, its heads faint, its rows a hairline apart; a heading alone (a card that names a group of cards, `<h2>工作 <small>项目和待办</small></h2>`) is 15px with its small words beside it, and a link alone is a tile (600, after an arrow); content of one line stands in the middle of a card one cell high. Links are in the accent's ink. It sets no other color: its words are the color they stand in, the page's text or the color your page gives the card (`.gallery-card[data-card-id="…"] { background: …; color: … }`) or a wrapper of the content (`style="color: …"`), every heading in it too, and its small words, labels, dots, quotes and lines a share of that color. Write colors of your own for both themes (`:root[data-doc-effective-theme="dark"]` on a custom page), a link's too where its fill is dark.
- Size: any whole number of cells, 1 to 12 wide and 1 to 24 high, 4 × 4 by default (2 × 1 for one link, 4 × 1 for a heading of a group, 4 × 2 for a few links, 8 × 2 for a short table): write `data-w` and `data-h` to fit what you wrote (`gallery page add static --w <w> --h <h>` does too). The user changes it by dragging the card's lower right corner, or from the 大小 menu of the grip at the card's top right, which lists the common sizes. The card does not grow with what it holds: what does not fit scrolls inside it, so give it the height it needs.

An empty one (`<div></div>`, what a menu adds) says that it has nothing yet (还没有内容, 跟 Agent 说要在这里放什么), so that the user asks you for it.

## How it looks

What you wrote, in the board's card frame on a page. The user changes it only by asking you: to change it, write the HTML in `page.html` again, that card alone, its `data-card-id` and place kept.
